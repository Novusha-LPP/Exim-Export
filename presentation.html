DEEP DIVE
5-Stage Movement Timeline Workflow
STAGE 01
Origin / Gate In
Container gated in at CFS / Port terminal. Tare weight & seal inspection passed.
STAGE 02
Vessel Loading
Stowed on ocean container vessel. Customs Shipping Bill & LEO released.
STAGE 03
Ocean Transit
Vessel departure & ocean leg transit. Real-time Leaflet map position updates.
STAGE 04
Discharge POD
Vessel arrival at Port of Discharge. Unloaded to container yard.
STAGE 05
Final Delivery
Destination customs clearance & container gate out for door delivery.
